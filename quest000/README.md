# Quest 00 – Ethical AI and Self-Reliance

## Summary
In this quest, I focused on using AI as a tool to **learn and improve**, not as a shortcut to get answers. I practiced attempting problems myself first, reflecting on my solutions, and then consulting AI for feedback or improvements I might not have considered. This approach helped me build a stronger understanding of the code I write.

## Main Insight
When code is written by me, it is much easier to explain and understand without AI. If I rely on AI to generate code, I risk not fully grasping its logic and may struggle to debug or explain it later. Ethical AI use means I try problems on my own first, use AI to explore alternatives or clarify concepts, and always ensure I can explain my solutions independently.

## Future Application
Moving forward, I plan to continue this workflow: attempt exercises on my own, test and reflect on my solutions, then use AI to deepen my understanding and discover improvements. This will help me strengthen problem-solving, debugging, and conceptual understanding skills, preparing me for real-world challenges where AI may not always be available.
