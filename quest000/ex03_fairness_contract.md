# Fairness Contract

## I will use AI when:
- I have tried solving a problem myself and i need some corections or fast debuging.
- I need help understanding why something works or who it works.
- I want to explore alternative approaches or ideas or add somthing in my existing code.

## I will NOT use AI when:
- I have not attempted the problem on my own first.
- I am taking an exam, test, or formal assessment.
- I am still learning the basic foundations and need to practice independently.

## I know I'm using AI fairly when:
- I can explain my code without help.
- I could solve a similar problem on my own.
- I feel more confident in my understanding after using AI.
